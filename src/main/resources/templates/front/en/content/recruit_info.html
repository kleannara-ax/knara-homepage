Request for return of recruitment documents

In accordance with Article 11 of the Fair Recruitment Process Act and Articles 2 and 4 of the Enforcement Decree of the same Act, only applicants who request the return of their employment documents are returned after confirming their identity.

Please refer to the information below to request a return of documents.
Job applications received through the 3rd-party recruitment site separately will be immediately destroyed after a certain period of time in accordance with the relevant laws.

  • Request deadline
    within 14 to 180 days from the date of confirmation of employment
    (recruitment documents will be destroyed after that period)
  • Return Documents
    Documents submitted by the applicant during the recruitment process
    * Excluding documents submitted through the website or e-mail or voluntarily submitted by applicants
  • How to request
    Download and fill out the Recruitment Documents Return Request Form [Appendix Form 3] of the Enforcement Rules of the Fair Recruitment Process Act and submit the Request Form via email
    (e-mail address: job@kleannara.com)
  • Return method
    Return requested documents within 14 days from the date of request
    (registered mail)
  • Inquiries
    HR Team Recruitment Manager (02-2270-9277)
