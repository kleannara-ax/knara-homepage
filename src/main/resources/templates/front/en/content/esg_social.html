CSR

As a compound word of Klean, which means the mission of KLEANNARA, and Cycle, which means cycle, it is KLEANNARA's representative social contribution campaign that means creating a virtuous cycle for a healthy and clean world.

Instagram

Sharing cycle

KLEANNARA listens to local community problems, overcomes them together, and strives for coexistence.

  • Donation of sanitary pads to medical staff at COVID-19 screening centers

  • Participate in blood donation to overcome the shortage of blood due to COVID-19

  • Distribution of free masks on the 1st day of mandatory wearing of masks

  • Donation of hygiene products to senior citizens living alone on the Lunar New Year holidays

Resource circulation

KLEANNARA introduces eco-friendly measures from product development and production,
and participates in resolving environmental issues in local communities.

  • Korea Environmental Awards 2021 Grand Prize in Industry-Greenhouse Gas Reduction Sector

  • Environmental cleanup activities, Eunjeogsan, Cheongju-si

  • Participation in Go-Go Challenge hosted by the Ministry of Environment

  • Employees participate in Klean-Garden to commemorate Earth Day

Social Contribution Campaign

KLEANNARA continuously conducts various social contribution campaigns for a healthy and clean world.

Creating a healthy world through profit return (Cycle)

  • Participate in solving social problems by inserting child abuse prevention phrases into products.

Creating a clean world through resource circulation (Cycle)

  • We are making wet wipes caps using recycled plastic to promote resource circulation.
  • We collect used wet wipes caps and make upcycling goods so that many people can experience resource circulaiton.
