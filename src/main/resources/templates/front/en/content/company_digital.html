Klean Nara has been taking the lead in pioneering the modern history of the domestic paper industry since
its inception in 1966 as Daehan Palp Industrial Co., Ltd. Restoring past history and revisiting unknown
records has enabled us to rekindle our hidden passions and envision a new future.

The rapid environmental change of the paper industry is demanding a new paradigm shift from us today.
KLEANNARA wants to build on the past 55 years of history and use it as a guideline in order to accumulate
the strength and spirit needed for the future. As KLEANNARA's founding spirit is being highly valued more than ever,
we are standing at the starting point to lead the 100-year corporate history with this as a foothold.

We will not forget that yesterday’s record is not only today’s mirror but milestones for tomorrow.
We will do our best to lay a solid foundation for building a 100-year corporate history for the future.
We will move forward with a vision of a 100-year corporate history of KLEANNARA, a new history,
a more beautiful tomorrow than today, and put it into practice.
