Our Approach & Policies

Basic Principles
KLEANNARA complies with the basic principles of business ethics that must be strictly observed in relations with all stakeholders, including shareholders, customers, competitors, business partners, employees, local communities and the surrounding environment.
  • Responsibility to customers
    We will fulfill our responsibilities to our customers with a sincere heart.
  • Fair competition
    We will compete fairly against our competitors and comply with relevant laws and customs with regard to our competitors and consumers.
  • Fair trade
    We will strive for mutual prosperity through fair trade with our partners.
  • Clean corporate culture
    We will develop a sustainable corporate culture by developing a clean corporate culture.
Necessity of Ethical Management
Companies are constantly striving to maintain continuity in relationships with numerous stakeholders.
Recently, in addition to the basic profit-seeking activities of a company, legal, ethical, social, and environmental responsibilities are being emphasized as important, and cases where the existence of a company itself is at risk due to failure to fulfill these responsibilities is increasing.
Therefore, 'ethical management', which prevents these risks in advance and returns the company's profits to customers, members, and society by reinforcing the meaning of the company's existence, is becoming an important management principle of the company.

KLEANNARA is making efforts to take root in ethical management by enacting ethical management regulations, executing a commitment statement of ethical management for all employees, and operating its related training programs.
KLEANNARA is taking the lead in creating an ecosystem of responsibility to customers, fair trade, and a clean corporate culture by expanding ethical management to not only internally but also to external partners.

Policies for good communication among
customers, competitors, business partners, and employees

Ethical
Management Policy
for Customers

Provision of accurate information
We deliver truthful information to customers, and we will not provide false facts or information.
For the safety of our customers, we will inform the customers so that they can fully understand the matters that require attention while use.
Customer protection
We will protect the customer's property in the same way as the company's property.
We will protect the confidentiality of customer information, and will not disclose information or use it for any other purpose without prior consent of the customer.
We will not engage in any behavior that harms customers' interests due to other immoral behaviors.

Ethical
Management Policy
for Fair Competition

Fair competition
We will obtain and use information legitimately.
We will secure a competitive edge through fair competition.
Unfair collusion is prohibited.
Respect for relevant laws and customs
We will compete in a fair manner while respecting the relevant laws and customs of the region.
We will comply with the Foreign Corrupt Practices Act.

Ethical
Management Policy
for Business
Partners

Selection of business partners
We will select and register business partners based on fair evaluation criteria.
When selecting a business partner, pressure or solicitation will be prohibited.
Mutual fair trade and evaluation
We will provide each other with information necessary for transactions at an appropriate time, and take necessary security measures to prevent damage from external leakage.
We will not change the mutually agreed terms and conditions without justifiable reasons.
We will notify business partners of the fair evaluation results and reflect them in the next transaction with prior consultation.
Sound opinions presented for improvement and innovation of transactions will be appropriately reflected in our work.
When using customer's technology or other assets, we will always obtain the customer's approval.
In the event that a customer suffers damage due to the company's fault, we will compensate fairly.
We will not engage in unfair practices that are prohibited by laws related to fair trade.

Ethical Management Policy
for Employees

Maintaining the integrity of KLEANNARA employees
We will act morally and ethically to maintain their integrity as KLEANNARA employees.
We will not generate distrust by starting or spreading groundless rumors, or harm the sound atmosphere of the organization.
Faithful job performance
We will understand company policies and standards, and faithfully comply with them when performing our jobs.
Reports necessary for performing jobs shall be timely, fair and honest.
As employees, we will clearly understand our missions when performing our jobs, and do our best to make profits.
Protection of company property
We will immediately report any significant event or potential occurrence that may result in loss of company property.
The company's property information is kept secure in accordance with relevant regulations, and prior approval shall be obtained for disclosure of company information to the public.
Company property information is only disclosed to authorized persons.
Business rules

Fair job performance and avoidance of conflicts of interest with the company

Maintain fairness in job performance by not engaging in the following acts.

  • Receive rewards from stakeholders
  • Lack of transparency in selection of business partners
  • Manipulate documents or data or file false reports
  • Wrongful equity participation in business partners
  • Illegal and wrongful use of the company’s assets
  • Negligence, poor management, acts without authority, damage to dignity, etc.

If employees unavoidably receive rewards from stakeholders, they must report them to executives and take necessary actions following their instructions, and report these facts to the ethical management office according to established procedures.
If employees are hired by another company, or end up performing jobs substantially similar to the jobs they performed at this company, they must report it in advance, and obtain approval.
Employees avoid acts that may be misunderstood such as selecting a company run by their families or relatives as a business partner, or influencing to make a transaction with the partner.
