Vision

KLEANNARA, we create a healthy and clean world.

“Truth is the fundamental spirit that KleanNara has been maintaining since its establishment in 1966. On this foundation, KleanNara has led the domestic paper industry and have grown into a company specializing in household goods. Based on the never-changing value of truth, we will pursue the right governance and grow together with our customers by creating a clean and healthy living culture”.
