Our efforts

Eco-friendly management performance

Resource Circulation Value Chain
Product Development We consider the eco-friendliness of our products from the product planning stage.
Purchase We fulfill our environmental responsibilities by strictly complying with environmental laws and regulations in all purchasing activities.
Manufacture Through continuous investment, we strive to reduce energy and water consumption in the manufacturing process, ultimately achieving the goal of reducing greenhouse gas emissions.
Mobilization We practice fuel reduction and greenhouse gas reduction by optimizing logistics routes, introducing electric forklifts and green certified packaging materials.
Use In order to reduce environmental pollution caused by product containers, we are making steady efforts such as applying eco-tab labels in containers that can be easily separated and discharged.
Share We practice various forms of sharing by actively engaging in employee-participation activities such as ‘Clean Garden’ activities to create green spaces and clean up the environment.
