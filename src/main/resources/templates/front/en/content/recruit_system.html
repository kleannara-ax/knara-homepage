Training system

Education system
KleanNara is dedicated to nurturing human resources with an effective and systematic education system so that employees can act based on their vision and core values and create the best performance.
Internalization education
Enhancing understanding of basic business processes and value chain
OJT education
Training the knowledge and skills necessary to create performance in the job field
Professional education
Reinforcing career and job competencies through acquisition of the latest trends and continuous development of necessary competencies
Education by position
Improving the ability of intensively cultivating the basic skills required for each position
job rotation
In principle, the job rotation system places right employees in the right place. When an employee requests for career development or there is a need for human resources within the company, various work opportunities are provided through job rotation in consideration of the employee's ability, aptitude, and development potential.
