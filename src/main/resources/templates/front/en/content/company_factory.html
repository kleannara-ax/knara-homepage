Cheongju Factory

A place where excellent products are produced through state-of-the-art facilities and professional workforce

Kleannara is composed of state-of-the-art automation facilities, professional workforce, and stable product production and management system based on the technology and know-how accumulated over decades. The Cheongju plant started construction in 1986 and is supplying the best products to domestic and foreign markets.

In addition, the household goods division has established itself as a local company that competes proudly with multinational companies by providing hygiene product lines such as the first release of toilet paper in 1986, women's products in 1987, and baby diapers in 1988.

Eumseong Factory

We will do our best to protect the health and environment of our customers.

The Eumseong Factory, completed in 2018, prioritized protecting the health and environment of customers. We paid more attention to the health of babies by thoroughly separating the diaper production facility, materials and logistics space, and automatically controlling temperature and humidity to prevent foreign substances from entering the product.

In addition, we paid attention to minimizing the generation of chemicals and protecting the environment by applying eco-friendly technologies throughout the manufacturing process, such as applying a new technology that compresses and discharges waste in the form of pills for the first time in Korea.
