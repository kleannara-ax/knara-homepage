Job Introduction

Business support

This job is mainly responsible for planning/operating tasks related to recruitment, placement, training, evaluation, compensation, and retirement. In addition, this job is making great efforts to improve the organizational culture so that employees can enjoy a pleasant company life.
The administrative department contributes to the development of the company and its employees through the maintenance and overall business support of the company's properties.

This job is responsible for treating various financial information generated by the company in accordance with the accounting standards (K-IFRS), calculating the amount of tax according to tax laws and tax policies, and managing the flow of various funds and expenses.

This job is responsible for innovating our business and performing tasks that sustain our company's competitive advantage by using IT.
This job is responsible for operating and managing essential systems according to the enterprise-wide system plan and architecture, and its related established procedures and operating processes.

This job is responsible for establishing mid- to long-term business strategies for the company's sustainable growth and preparing management policies to achieve the company's vision and mission. It also leads the company-wide business and actively collaborates and coordinates on major issues in each division.

Legal/audit jobs play an important role in helping businesses run smoothly.
The job of minimizing risks that may occur legally for the business that the company wants or is doing, of complying with internal/external standards, and of identifying whether it is being operated properly

Production

This job contributes to productivity improvement and cost reduction by performing various tasks to improve the production process and increase productivity by managing production facilities and personnel.
This job requires analytical and planning skills, understanding of products and processes, and good communication skills.

In order to improve productivity, this job perform tasks to promote optimal technology and facility improvement, such as improvement of production process, review of facility efficiency, new product/new technology review, and environmental/energy technology development.
This job is in charge of quality control in the entire process from material purchase to product production, and is responsible for guidance and management for quality improvement and productivity improvement.
This job is responsible for researching how to obtain the best quality through material analysis and quality comparison, and it is also responsible for working on manufacturing and supplementing equipment used for inspection to improve product production and increase accuracy.

Sales (Support) / Marketing

Business channels are divided into agencies, Nonghyup, hypermarkets, CVS, SSM, department stores, special sales, fabrics, etc. Through these channels, which act as the point of contact with customers, not only will business strategies be established by identifying customer needs, but the value of the company will be delivered together with the products.

This job is responsible for establishing sales strategies suitable for online channels such as online marketplace , home shopping, and general malls, and conducting sales activities by planning promotions and discovering new channels.

In order to sell products and services to overseas customers, overseas sales representatives research overseas markets, analyze products, establish overseas marketing strategies, discover potential customers, propose, negotiate, and conclude contracts.

This job is responsible for establishing the company's vision and mission, preparing a strategy to achieve it, and managing profit and loss.
The main objectives of the planning job are to establish and execute management strategies, efficiently allocate resources, manage profit and loss, and preemptively respond to the business environment in order to achieve the company's vision.

In order to support the sales activities and successful order receipt of salespersons, this job is to provide support for the overall sales process, and perform tasks such as closing daily/monthly sales and managing receivables, etc.

Through systematic consumer research and market research, this job reflects the results of analyzing market trends and consumer behaviors throughout marketing activities. This job is also to establish a brand positioning strategy for the company and its brand in the market and implement it to enhance the company's brand recognition which leads to long-term sales growth.

Research & Development (R&D)

Material development focuses on research and development of differentiated high-tech materials, and develops differentiated materials and technologies to maintain a competitive edge in the market.

This job is related to new product development collaboration and development of derivatives, management of mass-produced products, process management/improvement, etc., and takes the lead in stabilizing product quality and resolving problems in the production process through performance and structure review, improvement, and process verification of new products, etc.

This job is to contribute to increasing sales for the company's sustainable growth. In order to release new products in a timely manner according to market demands, this job is responsible for obtaining permits for the release of new products through understanding relevant laws and announcements, familiarizing with the licensing procedures at government agencies, and efficient communication with relevant departments.

Production support

Environmental management work protects the surrounding natural environment from chemicals by realizing reduction of water/air pollutants generated at worksites, recycling resources through waste management, and managing chemicals for the operation of eco-friendly factories.
The job related to energy contributes to factory energy supply through the use of boilers. Relevant legal knowledge, relevant professional knowledge, and ability to cope with situations are required.

The job of safety is to evaluate safety in advance from risk factors that may occur in the production site, reinforce safety awareness and conduct safety prevention activities through training, and manage the prevention system from fire/explosion risks for the sake of protecting human and material resources and creating a safe and pleasant work environment.

This job is to preserve the performance and function of electrical equipment by monitoring everything that requires electricity in the factory, and to perform operation planning, operation, inspection, and management to prevent safety accidents in advance.

This job is to carry out facility maintenance and predictive maintenance so that various production facilities within the business site can be operated in an optimal state.
This job is to manage all production and utility facilities required to operate production line so that production can be operated without any setbacks, and supports various maintenance/repair related tasks such as layout of production lines and remodeling of buildings.

The material job supports activities of production lines and sales activities with smooth supply and procurement of materials. Major KPIs of this job is calculating material requirements (forecasting) and maintaining an appropriate inventory according to the production schedule.

The SCM (Supply Chain Management) job is responsible for managing the optimal supply chain from demand planning, through production and distribution, to delivery to customers.

Through the establishment of purchasing policies and strategies, we purchase raw materials and equipment necessary for the plant at the right time, at the right price, and in the right quantity to reduce costs and consistently produce products.

Through storage, unloading, delivery (transportation) services and management of finished products, we establish logistics operation plans, derive cost efficiency, etc., and perform optimal logistics services for all logistics that occur between value chains.
